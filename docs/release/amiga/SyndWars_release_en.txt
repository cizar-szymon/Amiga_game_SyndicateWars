SYNDICATE WARS for the Amiga - release history
=============================================

BUG REPORTS
-----------

The Help key in the game: it saves the screen and the state of the game,
you give your e-mail and what happened, SEND sends the report to the port's
author. The version is in the corner of the main menu (AMIGA PORT 0.xxx) and
in the Shell: Version SyndWars. Without a network: describe the bug with
the version and add the file error.log of the game's drawer (the ARM
version: also RAM:swarm-log.txt).


@VERSION@ (@DATE@)
--------------------------------------------

- Bug reports with the Help key in the ARM version too.


0.220 (16.11.2026) - the first release (beta)
--------------------------------------------

- The whole of Syndicate Wars (SyndWarsFX) on an Amiga with a 68060 and a
  Picasso96 card: campaigns, missions, menus, saves.
- The ARM version for the Warp 1260 Extreme and Warp 7060 (Tool Type ARM):
  the game runs on the ARM, the 68060 shows the picture and plays the sound
  and the videos.
- Sound through Paula or AHI (Tool Type SOUND=), the music of the game's CD
  (MUSIC=OFF switches it off).
- The videos (intro, elections, endings, mission end) with their sound.
- Speed options in SYSTEM -> VISUAL DEPTH: FAR PEDESTRIANS, RADAR HALF
  RATE, AUTO DETAIL, SHOW SPEED.
- Esc asks before aborting a mission / quitting the game.
- Help: a bug report from the game (68060 version).
- Saves and settings in the PC's format - they go between the Amiga and
  SyndWarsFX on the PC.
- The game's data from the GOG version with pc/SyndWarsData.py (Python 3).
