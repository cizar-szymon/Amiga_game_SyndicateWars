SYNDICATE WARS - INSTALLATION STEP BY STEP
==========================================

What you need: an Amiga with a Warp accelerator (or another Amiga with a
68060 and a Picasso96 card, slower), AmigaOS 3.2, about 64 MB Fast RAM and
300 MB on the disk; Syndicate Wars bought from GOG (gog.com) and installed on
a PC (Windows, Linux or macOS) with Python 3 (python.org).


STEP 1 - unpack the archive on the Amiga
----------------------------------------

Open a Shell and type (Work:Games is only an example):

  UnZip SyndWars-@VERSION@.zip -d Work:Games

This makes the drawer Work:Games/SyndWars with the program, its icon, the
ARM version (swhost, swcore.wxt), the texts of the game (language), the
settings (conf), the drawer doc (these texts, the licences) and the drawer
pc (the tool for the PC). (UnZip is on Aminet: util/arc/UnZip.)


STEP 2 - make the game's data on the PC
---------------------------------------

The data (graphics, levels, sounds, music, videos) come from your GOG copy.
Copy the drawer pc of the archive to the PC (or take it from the ZIP on the
PC) and in a command window there type:

  python SyndWarsData.py "C:\GOG Games\Syndicate Wars" SyndWarsData

The first name is the folder where GOG installed the game (it has the
folder SWARS with game.ins and game.gog); the second a new folder. The tool
reads the CD image, downloads the three data packages of SyndWarsFX from
GitHub (levels, graphics, sounds), turns the sounds and the music into the
Amiga's formats and writes all into the new folder (about 260 MB, a minute
or less). Another language of the game: --lang ger (fre, ita, pol, spa,
swe); without internet: --packages with the three zips downloaded before.
With ffmpeg installed the music sounds a little better.


STEP 3 - copy the data to the Amiga
-----------------------------------

Copy everything in the folder SyndWarsData into the drawer
Work:Games/SyndWars on the Amiga, next to the program SyndWars (the
folders data, intro, levels, maps, music, qdata, sound and config.ini;
a folder that is there already gets the new files). Any way works: a
network share, a CF / SD card, a USB stick, an FTP program.


STEP 4 - start the game
-----------------------

Double-click the icon SyndWars. The intro plays (Esc skips it), then the
main menu with the version of the port in its corner.

The ARM version (Warp 1260 Extreme, Warp 7060): in the icon's Information
remove the brackets of (ARM). Sound through AHI: remove the brackets of
(SOUND=AHI). More in doc/SyndWars_en.txt.

SYNDICATE WARS - INSTALACJA KROK PO KROKU
=========================================

Potrzebne: Amiga z akceleratorem Warp (albo inna Amiga z 68060 i karta
Picasso96, wolniej), AmigaOS 3.2, ok. 64 MB Fast RAM i 300 MB na dysku;
Syndicate Wars kupione w GOG (gog.com) i zainstalowane na PC (Windows, Linux
albo macOS) z Pythonem 3 (python.org).


KROK 1 - rozpakowanie archiwum na Amidze
---------------------------------------

W Shellu (Work:Games to tylko przyklad):

  UnZip SyndWars-@VERSION@.zip -d Work:Games

Powstaje szuflada Work:Games/SyndWars z programem, ikona, wersja ARM
(swhost, swcore.wxt), tekstami gry (language), ustawieniami (conf),
szuflada doc (te teksty, licencje) i szuflada pc (narzedzie na PC). (UnZip
jest na Aminecie: util/arc/UnZip.)


KROK 2 - dane gry na PC
-----------------------

Dane (grafika, poziomy, dzwieki, muzyka, filmy) pochodza z Twojej kopii z
GOG. Skopiuj szuflade pc z archiwum na PC (albo wez ja z ZIP-a na PC) i w
oknie polecen wpisz tam:

  python SyndWarsData.py "C:\GOG Games\Syndicate Wars" SyndWarsData

Pierwsza nazwa to katalog, w ktorym GOG zainstalowal gre (jest w nim katalog
SWARS z game.ins i game.gog); druga - nowy katalog. Narzedzie czyta obraz
plyty, pobiera z GitHuba trzy paczki danych SyndWarsFX (poziomy, grafika,
dzwieki), zamienia dzwieki i muzyke na formaty Amigi i zapisuje wszystko w
nowym katalogu (ok. 260 MB, minuta albo mniej). Inny jezyk gry: --lang pol
(fre, ger, ita, spa, swe); bez internetu: --packages z trzema pobranymi
wczesniej zipami. Z zainstalowanym ffmpeg muzyka brzmi troche lepiej.


KROK 3 - dane na Amige
----------------------

Skopiuj cala zawartosc katalogu SyndWarsData do szuflady Work:Games/SyndWars
na Amidze, obok programu SyndWars (katalogi data, intro, levels, maps, music,
qdata, sound i config.ini; katalog, ktory juz jest, dostaje nowe pliki).
Dowolnie: przez siec, karte CF / SD, pendrive, FTP.


KROK 4 - start gry
------------------

Dwuklik na ikonie SyndWars. Najpierw intro (Esc pomija), potem menu glowne
z numerem wersji portu w rogu.

Wersja ARM (Warp 1260 Extreme, Warp 7060): w Informacjach o ikonie usun
nawiasy przy (ARM). Dzwiek przez AHI: usun nawiasy przy (SOUND=AHI). Wiecej
w doc/SyndWars.txt.
